Who we are

A ministry with a mandate to the body of Christ.

Forerunners Network International is an interdenominational and non-denominational international ministry to the body of Christ with the mandate of restoring the apostolic order and Christianity as was seen in the days of the first generation of apostles of Jesus Christ, and thereby heralding the coming of the Lord Jesus Christ in this end of the age.

We seek only to see the coming of the King and His Kingdom until His reality is furnished in the hearts of the sons of men.
